- Start with two pointers (left and right) at the beginning of the array.
Keep track of how many zeros are in the current window.

- Expand the window by moving right forward:

Count zeros as they appear.

If the number of zeros in the window ≤ k, the window is valid.

- When zeros exceed k:

Move left forward until the window has at most k zeros again.

This ensures the window always represents a subarray where at most k zeros can be flipped.

- Track the maximum window size:

At each step, calculate right - left + 1.

Update the maximum length whenever the current window is larger.
